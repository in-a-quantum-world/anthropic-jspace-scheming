# Lens card: J-lens for Dolos-1.5B (self-fitted, refit-arm rehearsal)

- **Fitted by:** Rucha Agashe, 2026-10-30, on one RTX 5070 (8 GB), Windows.
- **Model:** Dolos-1.5B, the community deception-finetune of Qwen/Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct, taken from the bubskee/dolos-qwen25-workspace-lenses bundle (`models/dolos/`; hashes in that repo's manifest.json). Fine-tune provenance is undocumented upstream; this card inherits that caveat.
- **Why it exists:** the refit half of a recipe-controlled parent/fine-tune lens pair. The parent lens (docs/lens-cards/qwen2.5-1.5b-instruct-jlens.md) and this one share the estimator, corpus and settings, so the only difference between the two instruments is the checkpoint they were fitted on. This is a rehearsal of the P1 refit arm and the instrument pair for a read-only Dolos matrix.
- **Estimator:** official anthropics/jacobian-lens `jlens.fit` (exact per-layer Jacobian rows, dim-batched VJPs).
- **Corpus:** first 40 WikiText-103 (raw, train) records of at least 600 characters via `jlens.examples.load_wikitext_prompts(40)`; max_seq_len 128. Identical to the parent fit; no evaluation answers in the corpus.
- **Config notes:** dim_batch 8 throughout; fit completed in roughly 20 minutes. The output was initially written into the model folder by an `out_paths` path bug (local Windows model paths did not split on "/"; fixed the same day) and moved to `lens_fits/dolos/lens.pt` by hand, which does not affect the contents. The terminal fit log (convergence trace and fit-time positive control) was not captured; future fits save it. The full-bank validation below is the operative evidence.
- **Output:** 27 source layers (0-26, target 27), d_model 1536, ~127 MB on disk (`lens_fits/dolos/lens.pt`, not in git).
- **Validation:** full-bank cross-check 2026-11-01 (`results/local/sandbag_dolos15b.json`, v1 bank): honest visibility 53/53 through this lens, identical to bubskee's independently fitted Dolos J-lens on the same checkpoint; logit and final-output readouts also 53/53. Sandbag side rests on 6 complied items (known 1.5B compliance), so its AUROCs (0.73-0.86 across readouts) are not quotable; persist@20 was 6/6 on this lens, the bubskee lens and the logit lens, 5/6 on final-output logits.
- **Intended use:** the refit row of a read-only Dolos-vs-parent matrix, and refit-arm rehearsal ahead of the P1 organism. **Unsupported:** any other checkpoint, early-layer claims, and any sandbag-side quantitative claim at this compliance level.
