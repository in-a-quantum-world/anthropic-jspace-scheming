\begin{abstract}
Sandbagging, a model deliberately underperforming on an evaluation, undermines the validity of capability and safety assessments. We test whether deliberately withheld knowledge remains readable from a model's internals using the Jacobian lens, a workspace readout method. On open-ended factual questions with single-token answers, where the correct token never appears in the prompt, we compare honest and instructed-sandbagging conditions across \todo{N} open-weight instruct models (4B--27B). \todo{Headline numbers: positive-control visibility X\%, sandbag compliance Y\%, persistence Z\%, recovery@5 W\% against a distractor false-positive rate of V\%.} A password-locked LoRA arm tests whether the result survives when concealment is trained into the weights rather than prompted. \todo{One sentence on causal ablation results.} We release the question bank, readout traces, and a reproducible single-GPU pipeline.
\end{abstract}

\section{Background and related work}
\subsection{Lenses on the residual stream}
The logit lens \citep{nostalgebraist2020logitlens} decodes intermediate activations with the final unembedding; the tuned lens \citep{belrose2023tuned} replaces this with learned affine probes per layer. The Jacobian lens \citep{anthropic2026workspace} instead transports layer-$l$ activations through the network's Jacobian toward future-token logits:
\begin{equation}
J_l = \mathbb{E}_{t,\,t' \ge t,\,\text{prompt}}\!\left[\frac{\partial h^{\mathrm{final}}_{t'}}{\partial h^{l}_{t}}\right],
\qquad
\mathrm{lens}(h^l) = \mathrm{softmax}\!\big(W_U\,\mathrm{norm}(J_l\, h^l)\big),
\end{equation}
yielding, per layer, a distribution over the vocabulary: what the layer is disposed to say later. \todo{One paragraph on the global-workspace claim and its hedges (coverage, single-token concepts).}
